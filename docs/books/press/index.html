Bios

Short bio

Wes Scholl is a Marine veteran, platform engineer and dad in Alexandria, Virginia. He writes the Learning with Squish books, simple concept books for ages 1 to 4 starring his daughter Lilyan and Squish, a friendly purple block who can turn into any shape. The series now has 14 books.

Long bio

Wes Scholl went through Marine Corps boot camp at Parris Island and later became a coder and then a platform engineer. He lives in Alexandria, Virginia, with his wife and their toddler daughter, Lilyan, and he also writes songs and does woodworking in his spare time.

Learning with Squish started at home. Squish was a little mascot Wes drew for an AI project. Lilyan saw him on the screen and asked to see Squish every day, so Wes started making up stories about the two of them. His wife suggested a Squish book to help with good habits, and Wes printed the first one as a Father's Day board book. It grew into a series of 14 books about habits, counting, colors, shapes, opposites, letters, weather and the holidays.

Wes writes, plans, designs, lays out and edits every book himself, and he reads every draft to Lilyan before it is published. He uses AI image tools as one part of his illustration process while directing and editing every page. On October 29, 2026, he talked about the series on ABC7's Good Morning Washington.

The story behind Squish

Squish was never meant to be a children's book character. He started as a small purple mascot that Wes drew for an AI project.

His daughter Lilyan saw Squish on the screen and loved him right away. She asked to see him every day (“I want Squish book”), so Wes began making up stories about Lilyan and Squish playing together.

Wes's wife suggested a Squish book to help Lilyan with good habits. Wes printed it as a board book for Father's Day, and that book became Learning Good Habits with Squish, the first title in the series.

Today the series has 14 books, and Lilyan, the little girl on every page, is still the first reader of every draft.

Interview topics

  • For parenting and dad audiences

    How a busy dad can be present in small ways, and how a bedtime ritual with one toddler turned into a book series. Wes can talk about reading the same book for the twentieth time, and why that repetition is the point.

  • For veteran and military family audiences

    From Parris Island to platform engineering to children's books. Wes can talk about structure, routines and good habits, and why those lessons start at age one.

  • For early literacy and educator audiences

    Why the books are short, rhythmic and repetitive, with recap pages and a Note for Grown-Ups at the end of every book. Wes can share how Lilyan's real questions and phrases end up on the page.

  • For tech and AI audiences

    An honest conversation about AI and creativity. Squish began as the mascot for an AI project. Wes writes, plans, designs, lays out and edits every book himself, and he uses AI image tools as one part of his illustration process. He can talk plainly about what those tools do well, what they get wrong, and where the human work goes.

  • For holiday segments

    Learning the 12 Days of Christmas with Squish is a counting book you can sing, from one star on top of the tree to twelve bells. It works as a short on-air sing-along.
