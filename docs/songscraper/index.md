# songscraper

songscraper is a small personal utility that turns an Ultimate Guitar chord chart into a
formatted Google Doc. Given a chart URL, it scrapes the chord and lyric content and creates a
new Google Doc from a template, with chords and section titles bolded and lyrics left unbolded —
ready to print or play from.

This is a single-user tool built and operated by Wesley Scholl for his own personal use. It is
not a public product or service, is not offered to other users, and has no signup or account
system beyond the operator's own Google account authorization.
